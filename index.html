Parking
A valid permit is required any time you park on campus. Purchase at any kiosk, dispenser, or via PARKbyPLATE. Daily max is approximately $15. Disabled placards park free in any accessible space.

🐜 Things Worth Knowing

A brief orientation to the place you're fighting for.

The mascot was a write-in vote.
In 1965, students were given sensible options — Roadrunner, Seahawk, Unicorn. They ignored all of them, organized around an anteater from a comic strip, and won by 56%. Administration was not thrilled. Sound familiar? ZOT.
Ring Road is a circle.
One full lap is exactly one mile. This sounds like trivia until you've been walking confidently in the wrong direction for ten minutes. Use the map.
There are tunnels under your feet.
Steam pipes run beneath Ring Road to heat the buildings. Nobody knows what else is down there. This is not a metaphor for university administration, but you may draw your own conclusions.
Aldrich Park has ducks.
They are not unionized but they are territorial. You have been warned.
Irvine is the most planned city in America.
Literally master-planned from scratch. Every street, every tree, every cul-de-sac. It is very safe, very clean, and aggressively pleasant. Our lecturers deserve the same level of intentionality applied to their employment.
